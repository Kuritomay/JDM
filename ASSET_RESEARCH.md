The original Miata creator identifies the model as their own 2019 artwork. Shixuan Li links that exact original and documents the licensed GLB adaptation. The AE86 creator page explicitly labels the artwork CC BY 3.0.

The generic Neill Bogie truck and procedural box substitutes are no longer rendered. Navigation exposes only the two actual models. The AE86 is intentionally stylized low-poly, not a photorealistic asset.

The Beat, Cappuccino, AZ-1, MR2 AW11, CR-X, Civic EG, RX-7 FC and Silvia S13 remain planned. Add their actual GLBs only after verifying provenance and redistribution permission. Do not infer asset rights from a repository-wide software license.

The Miata is relatively large (~18.6 MiB). Further texture/mesh optimization and real-device mobile performance testing remain necessary.
